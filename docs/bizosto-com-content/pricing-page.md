## FAQ Section Content

**Q: Do I need a credit card to start my trial?**  
**A:** No. Your 14-day free trial starts immediately with no payment information required.

**Q: What happens when my trial ends?**  
**A:** You'll be prompted to choose a plan and add a payment method. If no payment is added, your account enters a read-only grace period.

**Q: Can I change plans at any time?**  
**A:** Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes are prorated on your next invoice.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**  
**A:** Yes. Cancel anytime from billing settings. Access continues until the end of your current billing period.

**Q: Is there a platform fee for processing payments?**  
**A:** Yes. A 0.5% platform handling fee applies to payments processed through Bizosto.

**Q: How is tax calculated?**  
**A:** Tax is automatically calculated based on billing location and applicable local law.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**  
**A:** All major credit and debit cards through Stripe.

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
**A:** Yes. Data is encrypted, isolated per workspace, and stored on Firebase/Google Cloud infrastructure.
