# Client Portal Guide

(For clients who access Bizosto to view their account)

## Accessing Your Portal

- Login URL: `[your-bizosto-url]/login`
- Use the email registered by your service provider

## What You Can Do

- View invoices and payment history
- Pay invoices online through secure checkout
- View active projects and status updates
- Update contact details

## Paying an Invoice

- Open any unpaid invoice
- Click **Pay Now** or use payment link from email
- Enter card details on secure payment page
- Receive receipt confirmation by email
